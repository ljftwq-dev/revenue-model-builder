# Disclaimer

This demo uses **publicly disclosed** data from Luxun Precision's (立讯精密,
002475) annual reports, filed on [cninfo.com.cn](http://www.cninfo.com.cn) —
the statutory disclosure platform designated by the Shenzhen Stock Exchange.

## What this is
A **historical alignment** exercise: the model reproduces *past* reported
revenue via a driver decomposition. It does **not** forecast future revenue.

## What this is NOT
- **Not investment advice.** No buy/sell recommendation, target price, or
  rating is expressed or implied.
- Driver `share` and `price` values are **estimates / implied back-solves
  (C-grade)**, not company-disclosed figures. They exist to make the driver
  tree tie to reported totals, not to assert Luxun's true market share.

## Data use
- All figures trace to public annual reports; each driver cites its source.
- No large verbatim copying of report text.

## Audience
Research and education — demonstrating driver-based revenue modeling on real
disclosure. The authors have no position in 002475.
